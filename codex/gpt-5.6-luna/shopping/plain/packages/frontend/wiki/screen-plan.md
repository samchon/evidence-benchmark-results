# Screen plan

## Frontend review ownership corrections

The original requirement-to-screen ledger remains the complete 427-section inventory. Its legacy owner names remain as compatibility exports, while the reachable reviewed routes are extended by these active owners: `account-page.tsx` for credential/session/account lifecycle, `addresses-page.tsx` for address replacement, `cart-checkout-page.tsx` for payment outcomes, `reviews-page.tsx` for review CRUD, `seller-public-page.tsx` for public shop identity, `product-workbench-page.tsx` for seller product/media/variant/inventory/snapshot operations, `applications-review-page.tsx` for seller administrator applications, and `admin-review-page.tsx` for directory, grade, moderation, order, and category oversight. The backend refusal state remains the owner for unauthorized administrator access.

Every requirement heading has an explicit delivery decision. These entries map the rendered surface to the generated SDK-backed domain hooks; backend-only invariants remain visible through the resulting loading, empty, unavailable, error, and success states.
